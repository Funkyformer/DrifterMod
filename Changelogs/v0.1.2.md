# v0.1.2
## Card Changes

### Commons
- Pistol Deflect
  - Retiered to Uncommon
  - Renamed to Shoot Down
  - Cost increased to 5
  - Block Gained increased to 6(9)
  - Now scales with Damage modifiers rather than Block modifiers
- Steady Aim
  - Cost increased from 0 to 1
  - Now draws a card when played
- Take A Breather
  - Retiered to Uncommon

### Uncommons
- Bombard
  - No longer has Barrage
  - Upgrade now lowers the increased amount by 1

### Rares
- Overstress
  - Now grants Fabricated instead of Ethereal.

### Other



## Card Additions

### Uncommons
- Field Strip (0) - Draw a card and then exhaust a card. Gain 5(7) Charge.

### Rares
- Blade Flurry (2) - Deal 6 damage. The first two times it's played each turn, return it to your hand. It costs 0 and deals 2(3) additional damage this turn.

## Misc Changes
- Refactored how Modular cards work under the hood.
- Slight change to Fabricated keyword. Cards now exhaust at end of turn, even if they are not in your hand.
- Blades will now glow green when plinking instead of gold.
- Cards with a conditional "If this would cause a weave" effect now glow gold if they will activate.
- Gun keyword's "return to hand" functionality split off and moved to a new Keyword, Semi-Auto.
- Judgment now only adds Wracking Coughs to your Discard Pile.

## Bug Fixes
- Fixed text formatting for Roll With It.
- Rephrased text for Brace for better clarity.
- "If this would cause a weave" cards now function properly.
