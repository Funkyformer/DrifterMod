# v0.1.3
## Card Changes

### Commons
- Sticky Bomb
  - Explosive amount reduced from 8(6) to 7(5).
- Quickdraw
  - Base Weak increased to 2.
  - Upgrade now grants Fabricate+ instead of an additional Weak.

### Uncommons
- Desperation
  - No longer Exhausts.
  - Now draws 1(2) card(s), down from 2.
  - Now increases ALL other values every time it is played.
- Foundry
  - Renamed to High Caliber

### Rares
- Hotswap
  - Renamed to Foundry
- Overstress
  - No longer requires that the card does not have Replay.
- Possession
  - Damage bonus changed from 25% per Wracking Cough to a flat 100%(150%).
  - Now causes Wracking Coughs you Exhaust to be placed into your Discard Pile

### Other


## Card Additions

### Commons
- Explosive Round (7c, attack) - Gun. Deal 4(5) damage. Apply 9(8) Explosive.
- Planning (1, skill) - Draw 2(3) cards. Gain 1 Energy next turn.
- Repurpose (1, skill) - Exhaust a card. Gain 5(8) Block, or 9(12) Block if you exhaust a Status or Curse.

### Uncommons
- Armory (2(1), skill) - All players Fabricate, then gain charge equal to that card's original cost.
- Chain Reaction (2(1), power) - At the end of your turn, apply 7 explosive to a random enemy.
- Covering Fire (1, power, multiplayer) - Choose an ally. Whenever you play a Gun card or trigger a Modular effect, they gain 3 Block.
- Sidearm (1(0), skill) - Retain, Exhaust, Add a Pistol Shot to your hand.

### Rares
- Altie Form (3, power) - Whenever you play a card, gain 1 Strength this turn. (If it causes a weave, gain 1 more).
- Annihilation Beam (0xc, power) - Gun. Judgment 2. Modular 3(2) - Deal 4 damage to ALL enemies.
- Coup de Grâce (2, Attack) - Blade. Deals 4 damage. Deals 2(3) additional damage for each Weave performed this combat.
- Unyielding (1, power) - At the end of combat, regain up to 3(5) lost Max HP.


## Relics

### Rare
- Module Set
  - Description rephrased.
  - Refactored for better functionality

## Misc Changes
- Fabricated keyword will now properly appear on cards.
- Fabricated cards no longer crash the game at the end of the turn. Oops!
- There is now a Fabricate+, which creates an upgraded card instead.
- Semi-Auto cards will not return to hand if they are permanently set to free.
- Blade cards now generate 3 Charge (was 2) when dealing damage
- Modular cards can now be generated in combat, and instead have been specifically excluded from Fabricate.
- Explosive power now shows and updates the amount of damage that it will deal when removed.
